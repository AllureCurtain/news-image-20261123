A newspaper front page summing up Monday 23 November 2026's world news in one image: the COP31 climate talks collapsing in Antalya, the war widening across the Gulf, a Russian cargo ship burning off Finland, a deadly factory fire in Indonesia, and Mexican troops capturing FBI most-wanted cartel boss "El Chapo Isidro".

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies the image the repository holds to `/output/entry.png`. No network, no randomness, no clock — both runs write the same bytes. The image is a PNG, 1024x1024, under 1 MiB.

## What the image shows

The front page, headlined "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY — MONDAY, 23 NOVEMBER 2026", is built around an illustration of the day's biggest scenes: a cargo ship burning at sea off a cold northern coast, the Earth, and a flag-lined summit hall. The stories on the page, all reported on 23 November 2026:

- COP31 collapses: the Antalya talks end with no deal after the final draft drops any mention of fossil fuels, and the EU rejects the pact.
- War widens in the Gulf: Iran hits a US base in Bahrain, and a Houthi missile aimed at Saudi Arabia is intercepted as the Strait of Hormuz conflict spreads.
- Ship ablaze off Finland: a Russian cargo ship is still burning after an attack, and 14 are missing after a Russian drone hits a building near Kyiv.
- Factory fire in Indonesia: seven bodies recovered and 50 people missing after a blaze at a candy factory.
- Mexico nabs a cartel boss: troops capture FBI most-wanted "El Chapo Isidro" after gunfights in Sinaloa leave 11 dead.
- Also today: the UN agrees to keep negotiating curbs on lethal AI weapons; South Korea's education minister quits over an AI exam-cheating scandal; Nepal's PM meets Modi in Delhi; India posts a record 720/9 in the Wellington Test; the Democrats clinch a House majority of at least 258 seats.
